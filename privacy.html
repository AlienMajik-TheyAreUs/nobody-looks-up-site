Nobody Looks Up

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-30

Summary

Nobody Looks Up does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs other than the Apple frameworks it is built on, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Data on your device

Your progress (nights cleared, scores, settings, hangar purchases, badges, the collection, the optional pilot name you type, challenge codes and up to five sanitized error messages for diagnostics) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes it. If you back up your device, the save is included in that backup under Apple's backup protections.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center, which is optional. If you sign in, Apple's GameKit handles the sign-in and receives the scores the app submits; the app reads only your Game Center display name to prefill the local pilot name, which you can change. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off in the app's Settings at any time.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score or a share card, the app hands that text or image to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is between you and the app you share to. Nothing is shared without you tapping Share.

Children

The app has no accounts, no chat, no purchases and no data collection, and so collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
