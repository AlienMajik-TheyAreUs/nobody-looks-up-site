Nobody Looks Up

They never see it coming

Fly a flying saucer over farm country, lock the tractor beam on cows, chickens and sheep, and get home before the farmer finishes loading his shotgun. Thirty nights, a story with a co-pilot who has opinions, zombie nights, bosses, a wanted level, and a cockpit you can fly from.

Download on the App Store

Support

Questions, bugs, or a cow that behaved strangely: email Alienmajik@proton.me. Include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing when it happened. If the game logged an error, Settings shows a Copy errors button; paste that into the email.

How do I unlock Zombie Night and Last Light?
Clear night 10 in the campaign. The story explains why.
How do I unlock Endless?
Clear night 20.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save. There is no cloud sync and no account.
Can I start the story over?
Yes: Reset story on the title screen. It keeps your specimens, upgrades, badges and collection. Reset story + hangar starts everything from zero.
Do I need Game Center?
No. It is only used for the optional global leaderboards and can be switched off in Settings.
Controllers?
PlayStation, Xbox and MFi controllers work over Bluetooth. Left stick flies, A or the right trigger beams, Y switches the view, X uses your tool.
Challenge codes?
A code like QX7P2M-900 gives a friend your exact night with your score to beat. Enter it under Challenge on the title screen.

Privacy

The app collects no data and makes no network requests of its own. Progress stays on your device. Game Center is optional and handled by Apple. Full text: Privacy Policy.
